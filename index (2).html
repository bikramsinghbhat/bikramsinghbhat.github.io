RESEARCH

Published academic work

Bikram Singh Bhat, Nirjala Machamasi, Mohammed Abdulhakim Al-Absi, Khadak Singh Bhandari

Impact of Generative AI on Undergraduate Students: A Case Study of Kyungdong University Global

This quantitative cross-sectional study examined the effects of generative AI on learning behavior and problem-solving among 160 Smart Computing students at Kyungdong University Global. The study identified potential benefits including improved assignment quality, increased efficiency, and support for understanding programming concepts, while also identifying concerns related to overreliance, reduced problem-solving and critical-thinking practice, and occasional inaccurate outputs. The research emphasizes responsible and ethical integration of generative AI while maintaining students' independent learning and problem-solving abilities.
